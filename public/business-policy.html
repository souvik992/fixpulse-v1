Business Policy

Commercial model and usage policy for FixPulse Bug Tracker

Plan Structure

  • Basic: Free, up to 10 users.
  • Plus: Commercial plan for up to 50 users.
  • Enterprise: Commercial plan for unlimited users.

Commercial Usage

FixPulse is intended for internal business use by licensed organizations. Commercial use is permitted only within the plan and license scope granted by Souvik Gayen.

Upgrade and Downgrade Policy

Organizations may upgrade or downgrade between supported plans. Downgrades may be blocked when the current active user count exceeds the target plan’s allowed limit.

Self-Hosted and Hosted Use

Any hosted deployment, self-hosted installation, source-code access, white-label use, or resale arrangement requires separate commercial approval where applicable.

Restrictions

  • No redistribution without written permission.
  • No resale or sublicense without written permission.
  • No white-labeling unless specifically agreed in writing.
  • No transfer of source code ownership unless separately documented.

Support and Updates

Support, maintenance, and update commitments depend on the commercial plan or separate written agreement. Unless expressly stated, no SLA is implied.

Billing Policy

Paid plans may require successful payment through an approved payment provider before activation. Fees are non-refundable unless expressly stated otherwise in writing.

Contact

For licensing, deployment, pricing, or commercial usage approval, contact Souvik Gayen using the official business contact shared with customers.
